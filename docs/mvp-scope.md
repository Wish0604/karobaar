# LocalAI Hackathon MVP Scope

## P0 Objectives
- Merchant onboarding with role-based flow.
- Storefront and product catalog CRUD.
- Hyperlocal product discovery and comparison.
- Reservation and order flow.
- Paytm-style payment initiation + webhook verification stub.
- Merchant AI copilot and customer AI assistant.

## User Journeys Included
1. Merchant registers and adds products.
2. Customer searches products and compares options.
3. Customer creates reservation and merchant confirms.
4. Customer creates order and initiates payment.
5. Merchant asks AI for growth recommendations.

## Out of Scope for this scaffold
- Real DigiLocker integration.
- Production Paytm credentials and signature verification.
- Real-time speech transcription and synthesis pipeline.
- Production-grade RBAC and audit policy engine.

## Delivery Mode
- Modular monorepo, easy to extend into microservices later.
- One backend API and one AI service for hackathon speed.
